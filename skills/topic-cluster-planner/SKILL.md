C4. Topic Cluster and Content Calendar Planner
name: topic-cluster-planner
description: Plan pillar and supporting articles, assign keywords, and schedule a content calendar. Use for content strategy, monthly plans, editorial calendars, or avoiding keyword cannibalization.
Steps
	•	Ask for niche, goal, posting frequency, and any existing URLs.
	•	Build 1 to 3 pillars, each with 6 to 12 supporting topics.
	•	Assign one primary keyword per piece. Flag any two pieces that compete for the same intent and merge or differentiate them.
	•	Map funnel stage (awareness, consideration, decision) and content type for each.
	•	Sequence: pillar early, support pieces that feed it, quick wins first.
