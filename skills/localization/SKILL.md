G3. Repurposing
name: content-repurposer
description: Turn one piece of content into platform native formats. Use when the user wants social posts, a thread, a newsletter, a video script, or summaries from an existing blog or page.
Steps
	•	Extract the 3 to 5 strongest ideas, one standout stat or story, and one contrarian point.
	•	Produce the formats requested, each rewritten for the platform and not just shortened:
	•	LinkedIn post: hook line, short paragraphs, one takeaway, question or CTA
	•	X thread: 5 to 8 posts, each standing alone, first post carries the hook
	•	Newsletter: subject line, personal intro, one idea in depth, link
