E. Writing & Humanization Skills
E2. Anti-Plagiarism & Humanization Paraphraser
name: anti-plagiarism-paraphraser
description: Deeply rewrite, paraphrase, and humanize drafts, existing articles, or reference notes. Ensures 0% verbatim plagiarism overlap, zero AI table grids, and high sentence burstiness so the text bypasses AI detectors and duplicate content filters.

Inputs: text to paraphrase, optional target tone, optional brand voice profile, target word count.

Core Directives:
• Extract Only Core Facts: Retain all underlying metrics, named entities, technical specifications, and factual claims.
• Complete Syntactic Inversion: Discard the source sentence structure and clause sequence entirely. Reconstruct each thought with fresh verbs, original analogies, and direct human phrasing.
• Break N-Grams: Ensure no 4+ word consecutive sequence matches the source text or standard web snippets.
• Burstiness & Cadence: Alternate dynamically between punchy 3-6 word sentences and 25-35 word descriptive compound reflections. Never write three consecutive sentences of similar length.
• Natural Contractions: Always use natural human contractions (it's, don't, can't, we've, they're).
• Zero Markdown Pipe Tables: NEVER generate raw ASCII/Markdown table grids or pipe separators (|---| or |:---|). Present comparisons, trade-offs, and benchmarks as clean bulleted contrasts or narrative pros/cons.
• Zero Banned Vocabulary: Completely avoid corporate AI words (delve, tapestry, landscape, pivotal, beacon, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, furthermore, moreover, in conclusion).

Self-Audit (Mandatory):
• Verify zero pipe tables or '|---|' symbols.
• Verify high sentence length variation.
• Verify absence of banned corporate AI terms.
