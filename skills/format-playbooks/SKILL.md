E3. Format Playbooks (Short and Specialized Copy)
name: format-playbooks
description: Write specific content formats with the right structure: product descriptions, email newsletters, cold outreach, ad copy, landing pages, press releases, case studies, social posts, video scripts, and whitepapers. Use when the request names one of these formats.
Steps
	•	Identify the format and ask only what's missing: audience, offer, goal, tone, length, one proof point.
	•	Follow the format's skeleton:
Format
Skeleton
Product description
Hook benefit, 3 concrete specs or uses, who it's for, one objection answered, CTA
Newsletter
Subject line, one strong idea, short story or example, one link or CTA, sign off
Cold email
Specific opener about them, one problem, one proof, one low friction ask. Under 120 words
Ad copy
Headline with the benefit, one supporting line, CTA. Provide 5 variants with different angles
Landing page
Promise, proof, how it works, objections, pricing or next step, CTA
Press release
Newsworthy headline, who/what/when/where/why in paragraph one, quote, boilerplate
Case study
Situation, problem, what was done, measurable result, quote. Only real results
