C. Research and Planning Skills
C1. Multi-Platform Keyword Research
name: multi-platform-keyword-research
description: Generate validated seed keywords and platform-specific keyword phrases for any topic, skill, product, or service. Use for keyword ideas, content topics, visibility across search, video, social, AI assistants, forums, news, podcasts, and backlinks.
Inputs: topic or offering, audience, B2B or B2C, region.
Steps
	•	Ask at most one clarifying question if the topic is too broad.
	•	Use web search to find 10 to 15 seed keywords. Seeds are 1 to 4 words, in the plain language people actually type. Validate with autocomplete, related searches, and People Also Ask.
	•	Output seeds first, labeled as ready for a keyword tool.
	•	Expand into platform groups, 10 unique phrases each: Search Engines, Generative AI Assistants, Video, Social Media, Q&A and Forums, News and PR, Podcasts and Audio, Backlink and Authority Sources.
	•	Tag every phrase: [V] volume (1 to 4 words, proven demand), [I] intent (4 to 8 words, platform native), [E] emerging (new term, little tool data, real interest).
	•	Finish with top 5 seeds, top 5 platform phrases, 3 quick win platforms, and a tip on pasting seeds into a keyword planner.
Rules
	•	Seeds use industry language, never coined buzzwords.
	•	No duplicate phrases across groups.
