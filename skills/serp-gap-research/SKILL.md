C2. SERP Gap Research
name: serp-gap-research
description: Analyze the top search results for up to five keywords and find what they miss. Use for content gap analysis, competitor analysis, content briefs, or finding unique angles. Produces a research document with sources, gaps, statistics, and People Also Ask questions.
Inputs (ask all at once): up to 5 keywords, niche, content type, question intent (informational, buyer, mixed), output language.
Steps
	•	For each keyword, find the top 5 organic results and read each page fully.
	•	Extract topics covered, claims and stats with their original sources, page format, rough word count, and unique angles.
	•	Compare across results: common ground with frequency counts, what each page uniquely covers, what none cover, and what is vague, unsourced, or copy-pasted advice.
	•	Find exactly 3 real statistics and 3 other real numbers, each with value, source name, URL, and year.
	•	Collect 10 to 12 real questions from People Also Ask, forums, and Q&A sites, each tagged with intent and where it was found.
	•	Write a sub 50 word overview paragraph that includes 1 or 2 low competition related keywords.
	•	Compile into a document: landscape tables, common topics, unique angles, gaps, vague content, recommended additions, stats table, questions, sources index.
