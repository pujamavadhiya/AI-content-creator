C3. Content Brief and Outline Generator
name: content-brief-generator
description: Turn a keyword, search intent, and research into a structured brief with outline, word budgets, and keyword placement. Use when a user wants to write something but has no outline, or asks for a content brief.
Inputs: primary keyword, audience, content type, goal, target length, optional SERP Gap output.
Steps
	•	Name the search intent in one line: informational, commercial, transactional, or navigational.
	•	Write the one sentence promise: what the reader can do or know after reading.
	•	Draft the H1 and 3 title options.
	•	Build the outline: H2s and H3s, each with a one line purpose, the question it answers, and a word budget that sums to the target.
	•	Assign keywords: primary in the first 100 words, one H2, and the close. Spread secondaries, one or two per section.
	•	Mark required elements: direct answer block, comparison table, example, FAQ, CTA, limitations note.
	•	Attach sources to find, internal link targets, and the angle that differentiates from the top results.
Output: a brief the Human Tone Writer can consume directly. Let the user edit before drafting.
