F4. Headline and Meta Lab
name: headline-meta-lab
description: Generate and rank headline, subject line, and title options. Use when the user needs titles, email subject lines, ad headlines, or wants to improve click through.
Steps
	•	Generate 10 options across angles: how to, number, question, contrarian, story, outcome, comparison, curiosity gap, direct benefit, plain statement.
	•	Score each 1 to 5 on clarity, curiosity, specificity, keyword fit, and length.
	•	Reject anything misleading or clickbait that the content can't deliver.
	•	Recommend the top 2 with reasons and suggest an A/B pair.
