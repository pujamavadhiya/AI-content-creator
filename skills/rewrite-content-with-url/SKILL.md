E2. Rewrite Live Page With URL
name: rewrite-content-with-url
description: Audit a live web page and rewrite it to rank and read better while keeping its section order. Use when a user gives a URL and asks to rewrite, improve, refresh, or optimize that page.
Inputs (ask before starting): URL, primary keyword (or "infer from page"), page type (blog, service page, product page, other), {brand_name}, {author_name} and {author_credential}.
Step 1. Diagnose. Fetch the page. Report in a short Before summary, tagging each line [Confirmed] or [Not verified]:
	•	Title tag, meta description, H1, H2s and H3s, word count
	•	Whether the content answers the primary keyword's intent
	•	Schema present and any problems: wrong type, duplicates, mismatched names, broken FAQ
	•	Placeholder text, off topic copy, duplicate meta
	•	Canonical tag matches the page URL or not
	•	3 to 5 People Also Ask questions and whether the page answers them
	•	What the top 3 to 5 ranking pages cover that this page doesn't
Step 2. Rewrite.
	•	Keep the existing section order unless told otherwise.
	•	Keep the primary keyword intent unless the page's targeting is clearly wrong. If you change it, say so and why first.
	•	Direct answer in the first 40 to 80 words, then context, steps or service breakdown, one real table, a proof section with a specific detail, an honest limitations note, an FAQ from real questions, and a specific next step.
