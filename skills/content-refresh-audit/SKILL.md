F5. Originality Check
name: originality-check
description: Compare a draft against top ranking pages and flag overlap, copied structure, or too generic passages. Use before publishing, when the user worries about duplication, or after SERP research.
Steps
	•	Read the top 3 to 5 ranking pages for the primary keyword.
	•	Compare the draft for shared phrasing, identical heading structure, and the same examples or stats in the same order.
	•	Flag overlapping passages and passages with no unique value.
	•	Suggest what to change: a different angle, an original example, first hand detail, a different structure.
